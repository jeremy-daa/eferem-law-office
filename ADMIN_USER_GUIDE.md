# 📘 Eferem Law Office — Admin Dashboard & Publishing Guide

Welcome to the **Eferem Law Office Admin Studio**. This guide is written in plain, simple language to help you publish, edit, and manage legal publications on your website without needing any technical or coding knowledge.

---

## 🔑 Step 1: Logging into the Admin Studio

1. Open your web browser and go to:
   `https://www.elo-law-ethiopia.com/admin`
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In to Admin Portal"**.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you do not have an account yet, click **"Register Admin Account"** at the bottom of the sign-in box to create your login details.

---

## 📊 Step 2: Dashboard Overview

Once logged in, you will see your **Executive Dashboard**:

- **Total Articles Counter:** Displays how many articles are in your database.
- **Published Insights:** Shows how many articles are currently visible to website visitors.
- **Search & Category Filters:** Allows you to quickly search for specific articles by keyword or category.
- **Article Cards:** Each published article has 4 quick action buttons:
  - 👁️ **Eye Icon (Visible / Hidden):** Click to instantly hide or show an article on the public website.
  - ✏️ **Edit Button:** Opens the article in the editor to make updates.
  - 🗑️ **Trash Icon:** Permanently deletes an article.
  - ↗️ **External Link Icon:** Opens the live article on the website in a new tab.

---

## 📝 Step 3: Creating a New Legal Article

To create a new publication, click the **"+ Create Article"** button on the top right.

### 1. Fill in the Basic Details

- **Article Title:** Type a clear, professional title (e.g., *Navigating Foreign Direct Investment Laws in Ethiopia*).
- **Practice Category:** Choose the relevant area of law from the dropdown list (e.g., *Corporate & Commercial*, *Public Procurement*, *Taxation*, *Litigation*).
- **Publication Date & Time:** Select when the article should be dated.
- **Visibility:** Toggle between **Visible (Public)** or **Hidden (Draft)**.

### 2. Upload a Cover / Hero Image

- Click **"Click to Upload Featured Cover Image"**.
- Choose a photo from your computer (e.g., a gavel, office photo, or document image).
- A preview will instantly appear on your screen.

## ✍️ Step 4: Understanding Markdown (Simple Formatting)

### What is Markdown?

Markdown is just a simple way to format text using everyday keyboard symbols instead of complex formatting buttons.

Here are the basic rules:

| To Make This | Type This Example | What it Looks Like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Large Heading** | `## Main Topic Name` | Large Gold Heading |
| **Sub Heading** | `### Sub Topic Name` | Medium Heading |
| **Bold Text** | `**important legal term**` | **important legal term** |
| **Italic Text** | `*statutory provision*` | *statutory provision* |
| **Bullet List** | `- First point`<br>`- Second point` | • First point<br>• Second point |
| **Quote Box** | `> "Legal quote here"` | Styled quote card |

---

### 🤖 Pro-Tip: Ask AI (ChatGPT / Claude) to Format Your Text

You don't need to write Markdown manually! If you have notes or a draft written in Microsoft Word:

1. Copy your draft text.
2. Open ChatGPT or Claude.
3. Paste this prompt:
   > *"Please format my legal notes below into clean Markdown code for my website blog. Use `##` for main headings, `###` for subheadings, `**bold**` for key terms, and bullet lists for steps."*
4. Copy the generated Markdown text and paste it directly into your website's **Markdown Editor**.

---

## 📎 Step 5: Uploading & Inserting Images & PDFs in Your Text

If you want to include photos or downloadable PDF files inside your article content:

1. Look below the article details box for **"Inline Media & Attachments"**.
2. **For Photos:** Click **"Upload Image File"** and select your photo.
3. **For PDFs / Legal Documents:** Click **"Upload PDF Document"** and select your file.
4. Once uploaded, a **Gold Snippet Card** will appear below with a **"Copy Snippet to Clipboard"** button.
5. Click **"Copy Snippet"**.
6. Place your text cursor inside the **Markdown Editor** where you want the file to appear, and press **Ctrl+V** (or Right-Click -> Paste).

## 🚀 Step 7: Publishing & Editing Articles

### Publishing

1. Review your article in the **Live Preview Pane** on the right side of the screen.
2. Click **"Publish Article"** at the top right.
3. A success notification will appear, and you will be automatically returned to your main Dashboard.

### Editing an Existing Article

1. On your Dashboard, find the article you want to modify.
2. Click the **"Edit"** button.
3. The editor will open with **all previous text, category selections, date settings, and cover images pre-loaded**.
4. Make your changes and click **"Update Article"**.
